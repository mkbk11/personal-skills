## Practical selection

1. **Travel / one lens only:** Start with 24–200mm for the freedom to frame a street, building, landscape, portrait, or distant detail without changing lenses. Do not call 24mm ultrawide.
2. **Portrait / night street / indoor ambient:** Consider the 50mm prime for light gathering and subject separation. Balance f/1.8 depth-of-field errors with stopping down to f/2–2.8 when appropriate.
3. **Moving subjects / sports:** Favor shutter speed; a long slow zoom indoors may force high ISO. Avoid unrealistic "fast sport + dark gym + low ISO" claims.
4. **Perspective and background:** Camera distance changes perspective; focal length changes framing and the distance typically used. Do not claim the focal length alone changes perspective at a fixed camera location.
5. **Reliability and budget:** First identify the shot that is currently impossible or frustrating. Prefer practice or renting/testing equipment before recommending expensive upgrades; if shopping is requested, verify current compatibility and prices.
